# Terminology

## Canonical Model

Source-agnostic representation consumed by the analysis engine.

## Fragment

SquashFS fragment record containing compressed data.

## Churn

Degree of structural change between revisions.

## Reuse

Amount of filesystem structure preserved between revisions.

## Blast Radius

Estimated downstream impact caused by a structural change.

## Movement

Change in storage location between revisions.

## Root Cause

Most likely explanation for a detected change.
